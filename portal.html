Do this once. It locks your GitHub key with a password, so after this anyone you give the password to can sign in with just the password.

How do I get a GitHub key?
  1. Open github.com/settings/tokens/new.
  2. Note: a name you haven't used, like Classroom portal 4.
  3. Expiration: No expiration, or the end of the school year.
  4. Check the box repo, click Generate token, and copy it.

At least 10 characters. A short phrase works well, like blue-apple-river-42.

How to set up parent profiles (one time, about 10 minutes)
  1. Go to console.firebase.google.com and sign in with a Google account. Click Create a project, name it (e.g. Rabbi Eisgrau Class), and finish. Google Analytics can be off. The free plan is all you need.
  2. In the left menu choose Build → Authentication → Get started. Under Sign-in method, choose Email/Password, switch it on, and Save.
  3. Click the gear ⚙ → Project settings. Copy the Project ID. Under Your apps, click the </> (Web) button, give it a nickname, and Register app. Copy the apiKey value (it starts with AIza).
  4. Paste both above, click Test connection, switch Parent Profiles on, and click Save & Publish.
  5. Optional: in Authentication → Templates → Password reset, change the sender name to your name so families recognize the email.

Give families the Parent Portal address and the class code. To remove a parent, open “See & remove parents”, find their email, and choose Disable account or Delete account. They're signed out on their next visit.
